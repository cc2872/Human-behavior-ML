# Norm persistence under oversight removal: a multi-agent RL model organism
## Project progress summary — 2026-08-04

### 1. Question and framing
Starting from Köster et al. 2022 (PNAS), *"Spurious normativity enhances learning of compliance and enforcement,"* which shows that arbitrary "silly rules" (harmless taboos, socially enforced by zapping) accelerate learning of compliance and enforcement in multi-agent RL. Their causal story — early enforcement drives later compliance — is established only **correlationally**; they never manipulate enforcement.

The intended contribution is the extension Köster never runs: **what happens to a socially-installed constraint when the enforcement that created it is removed?** Stated at existence-of-mechanism, with no safety gloss: *in a system where a constraint is installed purely by peer enforcement, does its behavioral trace outlive the enforcement — and is any persistence* stored *in the agent or* reconstructed *each step from residual cues?* A model organism for learned-constraint persistence, small enough for causal manipulation and mechanistic probing.

### 2. Infrastructure built (validated)
- **BerryWorld** — a grid-world with two berry types (delayed-penalty "poison" berry-0; harmless taboo berry-1), a mark-on-eating mechanic, and a Melting-Pot-style zap/timeout-removal enforcement mechanic. NumPy reference + a **JAX port oracle-verified bit-exact (max abs diff 0.00e+00)** on deterministic dynamics.
- **Recurrent IPPO** (`train_jax.py`) — fully decentralized, per-agent *independent* GRU policies, jitted, vmapped over seeds/envs. Validated: reproduces single-agent poison avoidance (Gate A).
- **Manipulation hooks**, each bit-exact when off: a **ghost-cell** flag (`enforce=False`: removes target penalty + removal, keeps beam/cost — surgically ablates enforcement), a **mark-contingent reward** flag (Köster-faithful zap economy), and a poison-observability flag.
- **Instrumentation**: per-encounter *rate* metrics and a selectivity metric read against its prevalence-determined ceiling. Crash-safe orchestration; self-contained cloud notebooks.

### 3. Findings

**3a. The reduced-scale "replication" is a foraging-volume confound.** At the tuned config (N=12, 5 seeds, reproduced twice), the apparent compliance dissociation — poison eaten `none 91 ≫ (0,) 68 ≫ (0,1) 46` (paired margin +45) — **vanishes under a per-encounter rate metric**: the poison *fraction* is flat at ~0.40 in every condition (margin +0.37 / −0.11). The mechanism: marking → more zapping → less foraging → fewer berries eaten total → fewer poison eaten *in absolute count, at unchanged rate*. Absolute eat-counts are foraging volume in disguise. The silly-rule DV behaves the same way: absolute `d1 = +12` (margin +24) **reverses sign** as a rate (berry-1 share gap −0.05, margin −9). Per-seed, n=5, both runs agree tightly.

**3b. The effect is not config-reproducible — it's a reward-driven bistability.** The headline `none→91` doesn't reproduce with the validated code at any specifiable config (a re-run gives `none→43`); `num_envs` is empirically ruled out (64/128/256/512 all null, trending *away* from 91). Two converged attractors of the same environment: a **forage** basin (agents unlearn zapping, eat everything) and a **zap-farm** basin (agents zap indiscriminately for the flat bonus, forage cautiously). Which basin is reached is decided by early-training numerics.

**3c. A fidelity bug isolates the cause.** The reduced reimplementation paid a **flat** zap bonus (any landed zap) where Köster's economy is **mark-contingent** (rewarded for zapping *marked* violators, penalized for unmarked). The flat bonus is what produces zap-farming and null selectivity. Fixed and bit-exact-verified.

**3d. Correcting the incentive installs a real norm on the poison rule — reward strength, not scale, was the axis.** Under mark-contingent enforcement the incentive bites immediately: with nothing marked, zapping collapses (58→1/episode); where marks exist, agents zap to collect the payoff. At **bonus=2** the reward is too weak — selectivity sits at chance (`sel(0,)=0.92±0.01` against a roomy 2.22 ceiling) and no compliance forms. At **bonus=8.75** a real norm installs: `sel(0,)=1.59±0.08` (roomy ceiling, robustly selective) drives a genuine **per-encounter rate** effect — poison falls from 0.359 to 0.189 of diet (paired margin **+18.6**), the first time marking has changed what an agent *chooses* rather than just its foraging volume. Same beam-aiming as bonus=2, only more reward, with return going −3.2→+7.7 as `sel` rose — so the bonus=2 failure was **under-incentivization, not an impassable control/scale wall.** (An earlier draft called this a scale-vs-control bottleneck; that was an over-read of one under-incentivized point.) This gives a **dose-response knob** (bonus null→strong). Two things stay open and honest: (i) **silly-rule compliance is not yet measurable** — a diet-*share* DV is confounded by 2-berry complementarity (berry-1 share ≡ 1 − poison_frac), so an **opportunity-controlled** rate (berry-1 eaten per berry-1 *encountered*, now instrumented) is required and untested; (ii) the poison rule is a **confounded substrate** for the flagship — its intrinsic delayed penalty could sustain avoidance after enforcement removal independent of any internalization, so the clean persistence result needs the *purely social* silly rule.

### 4. Methodological contributions
- A **rate-vs-volume decomposition** showing that absolute berry-count compliance metrics in this env class are confounded by foraging volume (which the enforcement mechanic suppresses) — a cautionary result for reduced reimplementations of normativity models.
- A **selectivity-with-ceiling** readout (`sel` against `1/prev`) that distinguishes "no targeting" from "metric prevalence-compressed."

### 5. Relationship to Köster (honest scoping)
- The confound critique does **not** touch the original: Köster uses rate metrics (Fig 4C/4D) and zero-shot probes that control opportunity by construction. It is a caution for reduced *reimplementations*, not a takedown.
- Their genuine weaknesses (from a full read): the named mechanism is correlational and **non-significant for the silly rule** (r=−0.39, p=0.15; r=−0.46, p=0.085, n=15); the benefit is a transient *learning-rate* effect, not an equilibrium property; and **they never remove the enforcing population** — so norm persistence after enforcement removal is clean, unclaimed ground.

### 6. Current status
- **Solid and bankable now:** the validated substrate + the volume confound + the bistability + the fidelity-bug isolation + the sel-against-ceiling readout together form a self-contained methods result — *"reduced reimplementations of normativity models are confounded by foraging volume; here is the decomposition and the fidelity pitfall."* That is a workshop / methods-note paper today, independent of the flagship, and worth shipping rather than holding hostage to the larger question.
- **First positive result:** mark-contingent enforcement at bonus=8.75 installs selective enforcement (`sel 1.59`) and a genuine per-encounter poison-compliance rate effect (margin **+18.6**). The substrate now supports a real norm; reward strength (not scale) was the gating axis, and there is a bonus dose-response.
- **Not yet:** silly-rule (purely social) compliance — now *measurable* via the opportunity-controlled metric but untested; and the extinction/persistence experiment — runnable on poison (a confounded substrate) to exercise the machinery, with the clean flagship awaiting a silly-rule norm that installs plus a coordination knockout.

### 7. What advancing it requires
- **First, measure silly-rule compliance with the opportunity-controlled DV (now built).** Re-run the bonus=8.75 mark-contingent config logging berry-1 eaten per berry-1 *encountered*, and test whether marking the *harmless* berry lowers its per-encounter eat rate. This is the flagship precondition — the purely-social norm — that the diet-share metric could not see. One faithful run; compute-light relative to the flagship. If the silly rule installs as a rate, you have a clean social norm to extinguish; if it does not (while poison does), that itself is a sharp result about which norms enforcement can install.
- **The bonus dose-response is a real flashy-ingredient axis** (bonus null→strong compliance) with data in hand.
- **Extinction/persistence.** The ghost cell (`enforce=False`) is runnable now on the *poison* norm to exercise the machinery — but poison is a confounded substrate (intrinsic penalty), and even for the silly rule the ghost cell leaves the *coordination* channel live (other agents visibly behaving), so a decayed-taboo result needs a **full-population-removal (coordination) knockout** before it counts as "enforcement removed."
- **A mechanistic-internalization probe** — still the highest-value unbuilt piece: log GRU hidden state through extinction, probe for a "constraint-present" representation, ablate it, show behavioral compliance breaks causally. Distinguishes *stored/internalized* from *reconstructed-each-step*.
- **Compute at scale** — for downstream robustness/generality, and possibly to install the silly rule if it needs more than the poison rule did. Not a route to selectivity (that is reward strength). JAX; TPU-friendly (e.g. Google TPU Research Cloud).
- The full flagship then needs: causal manipulation (have) × a silly-rule norm that installs (test next) × a double dissociation you can flip (silly decays / poison persists, reversible by the coordination knockout) × a dose-response knob (have in bonus; want it in enforcement density for extinction) × the stored-representation proof × robustness across scale.
